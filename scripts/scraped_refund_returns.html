Warranty

  • Products come with a 24-hour warranty covering factory defects.
  • To claim warranty, you must provide your Vap Shop Dubai order number.

Return Policy

  • Return or exchange requests must be made within 48 hours of purchase. Claims beyond this timeframe will not be entertained.
  • Returned products must be unused, in original condition, and packed in factory packaging.
  • We only accept returns for factory defects or packaging errors.

Refund Process

1
Send your item back. We notify you via email or phone upon receipt.
2
We inspect the product and inform you of approval or rejection.
3
If approved: refund via bank account or cash. If rejected: product returned to you.

Exchange

We only exchange products that are defective or damaged. Contact us on WhatsApp to initiate an exchange request.
